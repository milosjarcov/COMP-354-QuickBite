# QuickBite

This is our team project for COMP 354. QuickBite is a made-up company that lets people order food online from local restaurants and pick it up when it is ready. Customers look through a restaurant's menu, put an order together, choose a time to come get it, and can check on it afterwards. On the restaurant side, staff see orders as they come in and update them as they go: Received, Preparing, Ready for Pickup, Completed.

We are building a small working piece of it in one sprint, using Scrum to plan and track the work.

## How we work

- Work happens on branches and goes into `main` through a pull request.
- Every PR needs one approval from a teammate who didn't write it.
- Done means it meets our [Definition of Done](docs/definition-of-done.md).
